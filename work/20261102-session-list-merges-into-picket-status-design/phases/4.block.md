## Phase 4 of 20261102-session-list-merges-into-picket-status-design: The work pane with Active and Done tabs, Peek, copy and rename

picket-status now has a work pane, opened and closed with `/picket-session`, which registers with `immediate: true`. The pane groups this project's sessions under NEEDS YOU, WORKING, OPEN, CLOSED and NO WORK ITEM. Pressing a row peeks at its work items, with items continued in a later session dimmed. A row can copy `claude --resume <id>`, rename the session, and move it to the Done tab and back.
