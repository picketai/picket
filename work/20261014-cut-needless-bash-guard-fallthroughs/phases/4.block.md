## Phase 4 of 20261014-cut-needless-bash-guard-fallthroughs: cumulative review fixes, round 3

Nested `"${...}"` of any depth now prompts when the quote scan cannot be sure.

**Critical.** A `${` inside double quotes is now matched to its closing brace by counting, and every `{` counts as an opening brace. Escaped characters are skipped. The command falls through as hidden execution when a quote or backtick comes before that match, or when there is no match. Counting every `{` keeps the checked span at least as long as bash's, so no nesting depth can hide a quote from the check.
