## Phase 3 of 20261116-in-place-guard-closes-three-open-spellings: Echoed and printed programs, and the limit bullet

This fixes both major findings from the cumulative review, following the approved D-001 (r2), D-003 (r2) and D-004 (r2).

The first fix covers interpreter rows that have no `programFlags`, such as python, node, perl and ruby. The guard now also reads a program attached to a flag cluster, so `perl -we'open(F,">src/a.pl"); ...'`, `ruby -we'open("src/a.rb","w")...'` and `python3 -Ecopen('src/a.py','w')` are denied. The second fix makes the guard read an `echo` or `printf` at any word of a segment, so an echo behind `command`, `builtin`, `env` or a brace group is denied too.
