## Phase 2 of 20261120-user-sets-the-cap-over-twenty: The stop at the user before the tester

When an item has more than 20 cases (six for each tested criterion), the test stage now stops at a `set_case_cap` gate before every tester send. The gate stays shut until the user gives a total that matches the six-each number. `picket reply <id> <n>` records the total and sends the tester, and `picket packet` shows the total and what it is made of. `npm test` passes, and `picket verify` passes.
