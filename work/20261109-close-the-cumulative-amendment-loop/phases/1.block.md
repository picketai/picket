## Phase 1 of 20261109-close-the-cumulative-amendment-loop: Mixed cumulative reviews reach the amendment gate

A mixed cumulative review now stops at the user's amendment gate. Before this change, a review with an `AMENDMENT NEEDED:` line and a finding on code went straight to the architect as a plain `amend`, with no reword grant. `planAmendStep` now opens `approve_amendment` whenever the newest cumulative reviewer row carries `amend`.
