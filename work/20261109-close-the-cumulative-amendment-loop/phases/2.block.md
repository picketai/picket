## Phase 2 of 20261109-close-the-cumulative-amendment-loop: An unchanged AMENDED does not answer a gated amendment

An `AMENDED` that leaves the record unchanged no longer answers the user's amendment gate. `route` now writes a `record` field (the sha256 of the artifact body) on every row that carries `amend` and on every `AMENDED` row. On the gate path, an `AMENDED` whose record equals the newest amend row's record does not count: it uses up the user's `[a]`, and the gate reopens with `the architect returned AMENDED but <artifact> is unchanged`. The `amended` plan adds the note `<artifact> unchanged since the amendment was asked`, so the hook's reply says so. Drift and phase amendments still count every `AMENDED`.
